# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-26)

### Feat

- **report**: count the findings in the terminal, without listing them
- **report**: show the screenshots of the PDF findings with --screenshots
- **images**: take the screenshots of the PDF pages that findings point at
- **images**: find the part of a text that a finding points at on its page
- **compare**: link the pages of the two documents by the text they share
- **pdf**: keep where the blocks lie on their pages, when asked

### Refactor

- **report**: give the order of the findings of the reports in one place
- **compare**: keep the place of the words that a side lacks
- **images**: share the checked render of an area of a page
- **pdf**: turn the boxes of a page upright in one place
- **pdf**: share the decoding of the characters that PDFium reports

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-18)

### Feat

- **compare**: compare long tables and code blocks line by line
- **pdf**: read ruled tables as table blocks with the pdf-tables extra
- **html**: read admonitions and add six generator profiles
- **spell**: use personal words and confirm the typos of compare
- **audit**: check the typography, style and spelling of documents
- **spell**: spell words with a bounded speller and read the prose
- **cli**: add the audit command and its reports
- **audit**: check the accessibility and the images of a document
- **audit**: check the structure and the links of a document
- **audit**: read the facts of PDFs and measure the quality of images
- **audit**: add the audit model and read the facts of HTML pages
- **compare**: match the images and show their changes
- **extract**: read the raster images and vector figures of PDF pages
- **extract**: read the images of HTML pages, set caption numbers apart
- **images**: decode, identify and measure images
- **report**: group the reports by category and add an HTML report
- **compare**: compare structure and links, group systematic patterns
- **extract**: set heading numbers apart and complete partial outlines
- **pdf**: extract and compare PDF documents
- **pdf**: build the blocks of PDF pages
- **pdf**: read the text of PDF pages as ordered lines
- **compare**: compare two documents and report their differences
- **html**: extract the blocks of HTML pages and sites
- **core**: add the document model, text utilities and configuration

### Perf

- bound the work of compare and extraction on large inputs
